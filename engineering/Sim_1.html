-First thing you need to do is create your piece.

-Shouldn't be that hard, it's just all the work.

-After that, you're supposed to choose a material.

-Considering it's all a materials analysis simulation, you would expect to choose one.

-In this case, we're using a lovely AISI 1018 CD, which was not in the standard SolidWorks library.

-If the material you chose is not in the SolidWorks library, then you might want to create a custom material.

-A method you could use to do this is copying an existing, similar material and pasting it in the custom materials folder.

-After that, you'll edit the ultimate strength and yield strength parameters, using the data you've collected from your materials.

-An accurate source you could use for this data collection are Shigley's materials data tables.

-After choosing your material, you have to create a restraint (Since we're doing a tension study).
